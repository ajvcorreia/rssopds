OPDS log

Every request the ereader makes to /opds, so you can see whether it is connecting at all and how far its downloads got.

Bytes are what the server handed to the network, not a receipt from the device — HTTP gives no such receipt. On books over a few hundred KB an interrupted download shows up short; below that the whole body fits in one socket buffer and will read as 100% even if the reader died.
