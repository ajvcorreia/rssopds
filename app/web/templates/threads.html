Threads (Meta)

Setting up Threads accounts as a source.

How this works

Threads has no RSS feed, and Meta's own Threads API only reads your own account. To follow other people, this app goes through ActivityPub instead:

  1. The Threads user must have Fediverse sharing switched on in their Threads settings. If they have not, their posts are not visible to anything outside Threads and there is no way around it.
  2. You need a bot account on any Mastodon instance, and an application token for it with read scope.
  3. That account follows the Threads handles you want (someone@threads.net) — the form below does this.
  4. Those follows go in a Mastodon list, and a feed of kind Fediverse polls that list.

There is no backfill. You only receive posts made after the follow, so a newly added account looks empty at first. That is how federation works, not a fault here.

Because the Mastodon API exposes in_reply_to_account_id, self-threads are detected exactly rather than guessed at — turn on Merge self-threads on the feed and a chain of replies arrives as one article.
